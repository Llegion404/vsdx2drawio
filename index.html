What converts perfectly — and what doesn't (yet)

✅ Converted as editable vectors

  • Pages, shapes, groups, rotation & flips
  • Fill / line colors, line weight, dashes
  • Custom geometry — arcs, curves, complex vendor shapes (as editable draw.io stencils)
  • Glued connectors (re-attached to your shapes)
  • Embedded images (PNG/JPG)
  • Stencil packs (.vssx) — every master as its own page

⚠️ Approximated or skipped

  • Connector re-routing: lines connect the right shapes but take their own path
  • Mixed formatting inside one text block (first style wins)
  • Theme variants, gradients, shadows, rounded corners
  • Splines/NURBS → polyline approximation
  • OLE objects / WMF metafiles are skipped
  • Old binary .vsd files — .vsdx/.vssx/.vstx only
